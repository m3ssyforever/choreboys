PRIVACY POLICY
Effective August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Two Fold Arts LLC, doing business as Chore Boys ("Chore Boys," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit choreboys.party, contact us, or join our text messaging program.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what you give us directly. We do not buy contact lists or collect information from data brokers.

  • Contact details you submit through our booking form: your name and email address.
  • Mobile phone number, if you choose to join our SMS program.
  • Message content you send us, including booking inquiries and replies to our texts.
  • Basic technical data our web host records automatically, such as IP address and browser type.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to booking inquiries, to send the messages you signed up for, and to keep the site working. We do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

3. SMS / Text Messaging Program

Chore Boys operates an SMS program for fans who ask to receive updates from us.

Consent and Opt-In

You may join the SMS program by submitting your mobile number through the sign-up form on our website, by scanning a QR code we display at shows or on printed material, or by texting our published number to join. In every case, joining is something you do on purpose. Consent to receive marketing text messages is never a condition of purchasing tickets, merchandise, or any other goods or services from us.

Types of Messages

  • Show announcements, including new dates, ticket on-sale notices, and venue or lineup changes.
  • Day-of-show reminders.
  • New releases, mixes, and occasional special offers.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. You can expect roughly 2 to 6 messages per month, depending on how many shows we have booked.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Chore Boys does not charge for the messages themselves, but your mobile carrier's standard rates for text messages and data will apply based on your plan.

How to Opt Out

You can cancel at any time by replying STOP to any message from us. You will receive a single confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and we will not send you any further messages unless you sign up again.

How to Get Help

Reply HELP to any message from us for assistance, or email us at choreboys504@gmail.com.

Carrier Liability

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is subject to your carrier's network and is not guaranteed.

Supported Carriers

Our SMS program is supported by major U.S. carriers, including AT&T, Verizon Wireless, T-Mobile, and others. Carrier participation may change without notice.

4. Sharing Your Information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only in these limited situations:

  • Service providers. We use vendors that help us operate, such as our website host, our form provider, and our SMS platform. They may process your information only as needed to deliver their service to us, and they may not use it for their own marketing.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of our audience, our band, or the public.

5. Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purpose you gave it to us. If you opt out of SMS messages, we retain a record of your opt-out so that we can honor it and avoid contacting you again.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email choreboys504@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act.

8. Children's Privacy

Our site and our SMS program are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

9. Third-Party Links

Our site links to services such as Bandcamp, Instagram, YouTube, Mixcloud, and ticketing partners. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page.
